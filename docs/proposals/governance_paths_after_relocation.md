## Situation
CookieGate moved from `S:\Dev\dev-S-path\cookiegate` to `S:\Dev\dev-S-path\Constellation\_DevRoot-Systems\cookiegate`,
and the Constellation service repository is now `S:\Dev\dev-S-path\Constellation\Constellation-Network`.

required pre-change reads (service map, registry, agent policy, shared invariants) cannot be followed as written.

- `CLAUDE.md` (items 1–4 of the required reading list)
- `AGENTS.md`

## Why not edited directly
These are instruction/canonical files and appear to be generated by Constellation tooling for every project under
`_DevRoot-Systems` (civicexposer, cookiegate, homegate, relay-tunnel, relaymesh, reversaltrack, rie, shadowprofile,
sitegate, themeforge). Correcting them centrally keeps all ten consistent. The Constellation registry identifies
CookieGate by `repository_dir: "cookiegate"` relative to the dev root; if Constellation's discovery expects projects
directly under the dev root, the registry or discovery root may also need updating — that is Constellation's decision.
